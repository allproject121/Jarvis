## Evaluation models

- `openai/whisper-large-v3`: independent Hindi ASR evaluation
- `theainerd/Wav2Vec2-large-xlsr-hindi`: preliminary ASR and alignment family
- `microsoft/wavlm-base-plus-sv`: speaker-similarity evaluation
- UTMOS-PyTorch: automatic speech-quality evaluation

Evaluation-model outputs do not become part of the released inference model.
Their names and versions should remain in the model card for reproducibility.
