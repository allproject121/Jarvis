## ⚡ How to Install on Android

1. **Download:** Click on the direct download link above or open [**Releases**](https://github.com/tiwariroshan121/Jarvis/releases/latest) on your Android phone.
2. **Install:** Tap the downloaded `Jarvis-Beta-Version.apk` (allow *Install from Unknown Sources* if prompted by your browser).
3. **Permissions:** Launch JARVIS and grant:
   * 🎤 **Microphone** (Voice conversation & continuous hotword detection)
